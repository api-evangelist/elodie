I’m excited to announce our brand-new games studio, Elodie, dedicated to building better cross-play games. I co-founded Elodie with my partner David Banks, the previous executive producer of League of Legends at Riot Games. David and I believe that games are intrinsically social and that playing with friends is the best path to enjoyable and memorable gaming experiences.
